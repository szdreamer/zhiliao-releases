## 简介（中文）

**知了笔记（Zhiliao Notes）是一款本地优先、开放存储、简洁高效的个人笔记应用，支持 macOS、iOS 与 Windows。** 它面向认真记录和思考的知识工作者：笔记默认只保存在你自己的设备上，无需注册账号，不上传云端，不用你的内容训练任何模型。

本仓库（`szdreamer/zhiliao-releases`）用于分发知了笔记的桌面版安装包与自动更新源。

### 核心特点

- **本地优先**：笔记默认保存在设备本地，不依赖云端账号，离线可完整查看、编辑、整理全部笔记。
- **开放格式**：正文采用 Markdown，导入导出自由，迁移备份不被平台绑定。
- **无需账号 · 隐私优先**：下载即用，不收集姓名/手机号/邮箱，无广告 SDK、无跨应用追踪 SDK。
- **端到端加密同步（可选）**：Mac 与 iPhone 之间点对点加密同步，密钥只在你的设备上；中转服务器只传密文、取件即删，默认关闭。
- **功能齐全**：富文本编辑（表格/待办/颜色字体）、图片与任意附件、笔记本/多级目录/标签/收藏、全文搜索、全局废纸篓、网页与公众号剪藏、一键分享长图、Markdown 导出、wolai 导入、三套皮肤与深浅色。

## Overview (English)

**Zhiliao Notes (知了笔记) is a local-first, open-format, distraction-free personal note-taking app for macOS, iOS, and Windows.** Your notes are stored on your own device by default — no account required, nothing uploaded to the cloud, and your content is never used to train any AI model.

This repository (`szdreamer/zhiliao-releases`) distributes the desktop installers and hosts the auto-update feed for Zhiliao Notes.

### Highlights

- **Local-first** — notes live on your device by default; fully usable offline, no cloud account.
- **Open format** — body text is Markdown; import and export freely, never locked to a platform.
- **No account, privacy-first** — download and use immediately; no ad SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
- **Optional end-to-end encrypted sync** — peer-to-peer sync between your Mac and iPhone; keys stay on your devices, the relay only forwards ciphertext and deletes it on pickup. Off by default.
- **Full-featured** — rich-text editing (tables, to-dos, colors/fonts), images & arbitrary attachments, notebooks/nested folders/tags/favorites, full-text search, global trash, web & article clipping, one-tap long-image sharing, Markdown export.
